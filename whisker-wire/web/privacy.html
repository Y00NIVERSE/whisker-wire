Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026.

Plain-English summary: we collect your email and password to run your account, your watchlist and notes to run Tick's features, and nothing else. We don't run ads, trackers, or analytics. We don't log what you read. We don't sell or share your data.

What we collect

  • Account: your email and password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see your password in plain text; it is sent directly to them over an encrypted connection.
  • Tick remembers: your experience level, the markets you follow, your watchlist, and any thesis notes you write, stored under your account so they follow you when you log in.
  • Newsletter: only if you tick the box at signup, your email is also added to our mailing list (via Buttondown), which you can leave at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Session cookies: two small cookies that keep you logged in. They are not used for tracking or advertising.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics.
  • No logging of which stories or articles you read.
  • We never sell your data.

Third parties involved

Your account and Tick-remembers data are stored with Supabase (database and authentication). If you opt into the newsletter, your email is stored with Buttondown for that purpose only. To answer questions and build the news wire, the server queries public sources live — Yahoo Finance, Google News, Bing News, Wikipedia, and the SEC — but this is done to fetch an answer for you, not to build a profile of you, and your identity is not sent to those sources.

Your choices

You can export everything Tick remembers about you, or delete your watchlist and notes ("Forget everything"), at any time from the Tick remembers page. To close your account entirely, email us at REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL and we will delete it.
