Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. We review this at least once a year and whenever our tools or data practices change; the date above changes when it does.

Plain-English summary: you can read the whole site without an account or giving us anything. If you make an account we keep your email and password (through our login provider) and the watchlist and notes you choose to save. We run no ads, trackers or analytics, and we do not log what you read. We do not sell or share your data.

Privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests: whiskerwire.contact@gmail.com. This address is the contact for personal-data matters under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). We answer within 30 days.

If you only browse

While accounts are switched off (the public preview), nothing about you is stored on our server. Your watchlist, notes and settings (for example light or dark mode) stay in your own browser's local storage on your device, under keys that start with "ww:". We set no cookies. You can clear them any time in your browser settings. Because we use no cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics, there is no cookie banner to accept.

Visitors and IP addresses

Like any website, the company that hosts it (Render) and the network in front of it (Cloudflare) can see the IP address of everyone who visits, and may keep their own server logs under their own policies; we do not control those, and they may process data outside Singapore. Our own code keeps your IP address only in memory, for a few minutes, to limit how many requests one person can make, and never writes it to disk or logs it.

Text you read or paste in Tick's reader (an article, an annual-report section, a link to a web page or PDF) is sent to our server so Tick can analyse it. It is thrown away as soon as the answer is sent: it is not saved or logged. Please do not paste personal data about other people.

Fonts are served from this site, so no font provider sees you.

If you make an account

  • Account: your email and password, handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. We never see your password in plain text. We use your email only to run your account and to send account messages. We keep it until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Tick remembers: your experience level, the markets you follow, your watchlist and any thesis notes you write, stored under your account so they follow you when you log in. Kept until you press "Forget everything" or ask us to close your account.
  • Newsletter: only if you tick the (unticked-by-default) box at signup, your email is also added to our mailing list through Buttondown, kept until you unsubscribe using the link in any email. Ticking it is separate from agreeing to anything else.
  • Session cookies: two small cookies that keep you logged in. They are strictly necessary, last only as long as your login, and are not used for tracking or advertising.

Why we use it

Only to run what you asked for: your account, your saved watchlist and notes, and the reading help you request. We do not use it for advertising or profiling and do not collect more than those purposes need.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics.
  • No logging of which stories or articles you read.
  • We never sell your data.

Third parties involved

Your account and Tick-remembers data are stored with Supabase (database and login). If you opt into the newsletter, your email is held by Buttondown. The site is hosted by Render behind Cloudflare. These providers act for us and are responsible for protecting what they hold; they may store it outside Singapore.

To build the news wire and answer questions, our server asks public sources live: Yahoo Finance, Google News, Bing News, Wikipedia and the US SEC's EDGAR system. Your identity is not sent to them, and nothing about you is kept by us. When we ask the SEC, we identify the site's operator, not you, as the SEC requires of automated users.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, using the email above. With an account you can also export everything Tick remembers, or erase your watchlist and notes ("Forget everything"), on the Tick remembers page. You may withdraw consent to the newsletter any time. To close your account entirely, email us and we will delete it.

Security and breaches

The site is served over HTTPS only. Account data sits with a provider that encrypts it in storage and in transit. If a breach affecting personal data ever happened, we would notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and the people affected as the PDPA requires.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will change the date at the top and, for account holders, tell you by email before it takes effect.

Not financial advice

Whisker Wire is an educational tool. Nothing on it is personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Read our terms of use too.
