What is Whisker Wire?

A stock-news radar for newer traders.

It reads 80+ independent news feeds across ten markets, ranks the stories most likely to move a stock, and shows how well each one is confirmed. Tick the cat then reads any article with you and highlights the key numbers, red flags and jargon in plain English.

Ten markets: United States, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, UK, Japan, India, Australia, Canada and Europe. Free, with no ads and no tracking. Education, not financial advice.

      How a story is scored

      points = top 3 signal weights
      + fresh (0 to 3)
      + other outlets (0 to 3)
      + ticker found (1)
      − 2 if the headline is a question

      • Urgent 8 or more points, and confirmed: two or more outlets, or one major or official source.
      • Watch 5 to 7 points, or 8 or more with only one lesser-known outlet so far.
      • Single source Only one outlet reported it. Wait for a second, or the filing itself.
      • Corroborated Two or more different outlets reported the same story.
      • Not in big headlines A strong signal that the big outlets (CNBC, Reuters, SCMP, BBC and similar) are not carrying yet. Early, but unconfirmed.
