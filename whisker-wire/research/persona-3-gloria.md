## Comprehension check

1. **What does "Single source" mean on a story?**
   Only one news outlet has reported this story. The site aggregates twenty-plus independent sources and tags stories based on how many reported them.

2. **What does "Under the radar" mean?**
   The story is not widely covered. It's the kind of story that might be important but isn't getting attention from mainstream media or the crowd.

3. **What do the big number and the "URGENT" label on a story tell you?**
   The big number is the story's importance score (calculated by the site's algorithm). The "URGENT" label indicates this is time-sensitive news that the site thinks you should know about *now*, not later.

4. **What does "Corroborated" mean?**
   Multiple news sources reported the same story. If a story is "Corroborated," it means two or more independent outlets covered it, so it's less likely to be a false or distorted report.

5. **What does the Value Radar score mean, and is a high score a "buy" signal?**
   The score (0–100) rates how undervalued a stock is based on discount to 52-week high, forward earnings, book value, analyst consensus, and unusual volume. A high score means the stock *looks cheap by these metrics*, not that you should buy it. The site's own words: "Cheap is a question, not an answer." You still need to do your own analysis.

6. **What is Tick's job, and how much should you trust her highlights?**
   Tick is an AI that reads articles and spots patterns—keywords, numbers, financial concepts. She highlights the sentences she thinks matter and explains why. But she explicitly says "She spots patterns, not truth: verify anything you might act on." Trust her for direction, not for conclusions. She's a research assistant, not an advisor.

7. **In one sentence: what is this site's promise, and do you believe it?**
   The promise is to show "what moved in the last hours, ranked by how much it should matter" with full transparency about sources and methodology. I believe the intent, but I need to verify the execution (I couldn't fully test source access and I didn't find source toggles).
