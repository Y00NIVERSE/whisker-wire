    Questions from the mailroom

    Does the card check my Patreon automatically?

    Not yet — it runs on the honour system. You tell it when you joined, it counts the months, and it gives you a claim code. When you send that code I cross-check it against your membership on Patreon before your reward goes out, so the count needs to be real.

    What counts as a stamp?

    One stamp per month of active membership, starting with the month you joined. Every 5 stamps is a reward: stamp 5 is a free die-cut sticker, stamp 10 is 50% off the online shop plus a free sticker sheet. After 10 the card starts a fresh cycle and the rewards come round again.

    I paused my membership — what happens?

    Use the manual nudge to take off the months you were away, or just message me and I’ll sort your count out. Paused months don’t earn stamps, but nothing you’ve already claimed disappears.

    Will I lose my card if I change device?

    The card is saved in this browser only. On a new phone or laptop, just enter your start month again — the stamp count is worked out from the date, so it’ll catch right back up.
